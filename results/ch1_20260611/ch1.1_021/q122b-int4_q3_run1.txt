Here's a thinking process that leads to the solution:

1.  **Understand the Goal:** The objective is to find the meeting time and the meeting location (distance from City A) for two trains traveling towards each other.

2.  **Identify the Given Information:**
    *   **Train 1 (from City A):**
        *   Departure Time: 9:00
        *   Speed ($v_1$): 60 km/h
        *   Direction: A $\rightarrow$ B
    *   **Train 2 (from City B):**
        *   Departure Time: 9:30
        *   Speed ($v_2$): 80 km/h
        *   Direction: B $\rightarrow$ A
    *   **Total Distance ($D$):** 280 km
    *   **Route:** Same line (straight path implied).

3.  **Determine the Strategy:**
    *   Calculate how far Train 1 travels before Train 2 starts.
    *   Calculate the remaining distance between the two trains when Train 2 starts.
    *   Calculate the relative speed (closing speed) of the two trains.
    *   Calculate the time it takes for them to meet after Train 2 starts.
    *   Add this time to Train 2's start time to get the meeting clock time.
    *   Calculate the total distance Train 1 has traveled from City A to find the meeting location.

4.  **Step-by-Step Execution:**

    *   **Step 1: Analyze the head start.**
        *   Train 1 starts at 9:00.
        *   Train 2 starts at 9:30.
        *   Time difference ($\Delta t$) = 30 minutes = 0.5 hours.
        *   Distance Train 1 travels in this time ($d_{headstart}$):
            $$d_{headstart} = v_1 \times \Delta t$$
            $$d_{headstart} = 60 \text{ km/h} \times 0.5 \text{ h} = 30 \text{ km}$$

    *   **Step 2: Determine the remaining distance.**
        *   Total distance ($D$) = 280 km.
        *   Distance covered by Train 1 before Train 2 moves = 30 km.
        *   Remaining distance ($D_{remaining}$) between the two trains at 9:30:
            $$D_{remaining} = 280 \text{ km} - 30 \text{ km} = 250 \text{ km}$$

    *   **Step 3: Calculate the time to meet after 9:30.**
        *   Now both trains are moving towards each other.
        *   Speed of Train 1 ($v_1$) = 60 km/h.
        *   Speed of Train 2 ($v_2$) = 80 km/h.
        *   Relative speed ($v_{rel}$) = $v_1 + v_2$ (since they are moving towards each other).
            $$v_{rel} = 60 + 80 = 140 \text{ km/h}$$
        *   Time to meet ($t_{meet}$) = $\frac{D_{remaining}}{v_{rel}}$
            $$t_{meet} = \frac{250 \text{ km}}{140 \text{ km/h}}$$
            $$t_{meet} = \frac{25}{14} \text{ hours}$$
