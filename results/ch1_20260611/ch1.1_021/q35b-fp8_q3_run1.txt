Here's a thinking process that leads to the solution:

1.  **Understand the Goal:** The objective is to find the time and location (distance from City A) where two trains meet.

2.  **Identify the Given Information:**
    *   **Train 1 (from A):**
        *   Departure time: 9:00
        *   Speed ($v_1$): 60 km/h
        *   Direction: A $\to$ B
    *   **Train 2 (from B):**
        *   Departure time: 9:30
        *   Speed ($v_2$): 80 km/h
        *   Direction: B $\to$ A
    *   **Distance between cities ($D$):** 280 km

3.  **Analyze the Timeline:**
    *   Train 1 starts at 9:00.
    *   Train 2 starts at 9:30.
    *   There is a 30-minute (0.5 hour) head start for Train 1.

4.  **Step-by-Step Calculation Strategy:**

    *   *Step 1: Calculate the distance covered by Train 1 before Train 2 starts.*
    *   *Step 2: Determine the remaining distance between the two trains at 9:30.*
    *   *Step 3: Calculate the time it takes for the two trains to meet after 9:30 (using relative speed).*
    *   *Step 4: Determine the total time elapsed from 9:00 to find the meeting clock time.*
    *   *Step 5: Calculate the distance from City A (using Train 1's total travel time or distance).*

5.  **Execution:**

    *   **Step 1: Head start distance.**
        *   Time difference = 9:30 - 9:00 = 30 minutes = 0.5 hours.
        *   Speed of Train 1 = 60 km/h.
        *   Distance covered ($d_{head}$) = Speed $\times$ Time = $60 \times 0.5 = 30$ km.

    *   **Step 2: Remaining distance.**
        *   Total distance = 280 km.
        *   Distance covered by Train 1 = 30 km.
        *   Remaining distance ($d_{rem}$) = $280 - 30 = 250$ km.
        *   *Self-Correction/Check:* At 9:30, Train 1 is 30 km from A. Train 2 is at B (280 km from A). Distance between them is $280 - 30 = 250$ km. Correct.

    *   **Step 3: Time to meet after 9:30.**
        *   The trains are moving towards each other.
        *   Relative speed ($v_{rel}$) = $v_1 + v_2$ (since they are approaching).
        *   $v_{rel} = 60 + 80 = 140$ km/h.
        *   Distance to close = 250 km.
        *   Time ($t_{meet}$) = Distance / Relative Speed = $250 / 140$.
        *   Calculation: $250 / 140 = 25 / 14$ hours.
        *   Convert to hours and minutes:
            *   $25 / 14 \approx 1.7857$ hours.
            *   Integer hours = 1 hour.
            *   Remaining fraction = $11/14$ hours.
            *   Minutes = $(11/14) \times 60$.
            *   $11 \times 60 = 660$.
            *   $660 / 14 = 330 / 7 \approx 47.14$ minutes.
        *   *Alternative approach for Step 3 (Algebraic):* Let $t$ be the time *after* 9:30.
            *   Distance Train 1 travels in $t$ hours = $60t$.
            *   Distance Train 2 travels in $t$ hours = $80t$.
            *   Sum of distances + Head start distance = Total Distance.
            *   $30 + 60t + 80t = 280$.
            *   $140t = 250$.
