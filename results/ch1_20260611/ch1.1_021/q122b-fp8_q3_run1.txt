Here's a thinking process that leads to the solution:

1.  **Understand the Goal:** The objective is to find the meeting time and the meeting location (distance from City A) for two trains traveling towards each other.

2.  **Identify the Given Information:**
    *   **Train 1 (from City A):**
        *   Departure Time: 9:00
        *   Speed ($v_1$): 60 km/h
        *   Direction: A $\rightarrow$ B
    *   **Train 2 (from City B):**
        *   Departure Time: 9:30
        *   Speed ($v_2$): 80 km/h
        *   Direction: B $\rightarrow$ A
    *   **Total Distance ($D$):** 280 km

3.  **Determine the Strategy:**
    *   Since the trains leave at different times, I need to account for the head start of Train 1.
    *   Calculate how far Train 1 travels before Train 2 starts.
    *   Calculate the remaining distance between them when Train 2 starts.
    *   Calculate the time it takes for them to cover the remaining distance together (relative speed).
    *   Add this travel time to the start time of Train 2 to get the meeting time.
    *   Calculate the total distance Train 1 has traveled from City A to find the meeting location.

4.  **Step-by-Step Calculation:**

    *   **Step 1: Calculate Train 1's head start.**
        *   Time difference between departures: 9:30 - 9:00 = 30 minutes.
        *   Convert minutes to hours: $30 \text{ min} = 0.5 \text{ hours}$.
        *   Distance traveled by Train 1 in this time ($d_{headstart}$):
            $$d_{headstart} = \text{Speed}_1 \times \text{Time}$$
            $$d_{headstart} = 60 \text{ km/h} \times 0.5 \text{ h} = 30 \text{ km}$$

    *   **Step 2: Calculate the remaining distance.**
        *   Total distance ($D$) = 280 km.
        *   Distance covered by Train 1 before Train 2 leaves = 30 km.
        *   Remaining distance ($D_{remaining}$) = $280 - 30 = 250 \text{ km}$.
        *   *Note:* At 9:30, Train 1 is 30 km from A, and Train 2 is at B (280 km from A). The gap between them is 250 km.

    *   **Step 3: Calculate the relative speed.**
        *   Since they are moving towards each other, their speeds add up.
        *   Relative Speed ($v_{rel}$) = $v_1 + v_2$
        *   $v_{rel} = 60 \text{ km/h} + 80 \text{ km/h} = 140 \text{ km/h}$.

    *   **Step 4: Calculate the time to meet after 9:30.**
        *   Time ($t_{meet}$) = $\frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Speed}}$
        *   $t_{meet} = \frac{D_{remaining}}{v_{rel}}$
        *   $t_{meet} = \frac{250 \text{ km}}{140 \text{ km/h}}$
