### Step 1: Determine the position of the first train when the second train starts

*   **Distance covered by Train A:** $\text{Speed} \times \text{Time} = 60 \text{ km/h} \times 0.5 \text{ h} = 30 \text{ km}$.

### Step 2: Calculate the remaining distance between the trains at 9:30
At 9:30, Train A has already traveled 30 km toward City B.
*   **Total distance:** 280 km.
*   **Remaining distance:** $280 \text{ km} - 30 \text{ km} = 250 \text{ km}$.

### Step 3: Determine the relative speed of the two trains
Since the trains are traveling toward each other, their speeds are additive.
*   **Relative Speed:** $60 \text{ km/h} + 80 \text{ km/h} = 140 \text{ km/h}$.

Using the remaining distance and the relative speed:

*   $\text{Time} = \frac{25}{14} \text{ hours} \approx 1.7857 \text{ hours}$.
