2.  **Identify the Given Information:**
    *   **Train 1 (from A):**
        *   Departure time: 9:00
        *   Speed ($v_1$): 60 km/h
        *   Direction: A $\to$ B
    *   **Train 2 (from B):**
        *   Departure time: 9:30
        *   Speed ($v_2$): 80 km/h
        *   Direction: B $\to$ A
    *   **Distance between cities ($D$):** 280 km

3.  **Analyze the Timeline:**
    *   Train 1 starts at 9:00.
    *   Train 2 starts at 9:30.
    *   There is a 30-minute (0.5 hour) head start for Train 1.

4.  **Step-by-Step Calculation Strategy:**

    *   *Step 1: Calculate the distance covered by Train 1 before Train 2 starts.*
    *   *Step 2: Determine the remaining distance between the two trains when Train 2 starts.*
    *   *Step 3: Calculate the time it takes for both trains to meet after Train 2 starts (using relative speed).*
    *   *Step 4: Determine the total time elapsed from 9:00 to find the meeting clock time.*
    *   *Step 5: Calculate the distance from City A (using Train 1's total travel time or distance).*

5.  **Execution:**

        *   Time difference = 9:30 - 9:00 = 30 minutes = 0.5 hours.

        *   Distance covered ($d_{head}$) = Speed $\times$ Time = $60 \times 0.5 = 30$ km.

    *   **Step 2: Remaining distance.**

        *   Distance covered by Train 1 = 30 km.
